Cursor IPC regression checks: `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_companion_cursor.py -v`. These use a temporary fake compositor socket and a windowless test shell. They cover fragmented/malformed replies, a deadline across trickled reads, rate limiting, unchanged samples, owner shutdown, slow-peer recovery, full output pipes, and a 500 ms busy GUI. The old QML socket left the fake compositor waiting approximately 500 ms for its request; the worker delivered it in under 1 ms during the same stall. This verifies removal of the GUI-dependent request path, not every possible cause of a live display freeze. Hyprland v0.56.2's [control callback](https://github.com/hyprwm/Hyprland/blob/efb50993780079460b0cbed1363e2166a2de1d9f/src/debug/HyprCtl.cpp#L2232) waits synchronously for an accepted client's request, which is why the connection and immediate send now belong to the worker.
