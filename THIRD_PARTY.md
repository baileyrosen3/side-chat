Model and voice licenses are separate from the runtime code licenses. Restricted noncommercial voices in other upstream datasets are not offered by Peek. Voice states and quantized/converted model files are adaptations supplied by their credited upstream projects. SHA-256 records for downloaded model artifacts are saved locally in models/inventory.json. No cloud speech service is used.

The user's chosen CLI and model provider retain their own licenses and account terms. In particular, Claude is not an open-source dependency of this plugin; it is an optional installed-agent integration. Peek does not change providers or credentials.
